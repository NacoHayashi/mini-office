Furniture Kit
by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)

License: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These GLB files are models from Kenney's Furniture Kit (official download
at kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit):
desk.glb, chairDesk.glb, chairRounded.glb, loungeSofa.glb, table.glb,
tableCoffee.glb, bookcaseOpen.glb, bookcaseOpenLow.glb, books.glb,
pottedPlant.glb, plantSmall1.glb, lampSquareFloor.glb, televisionModern.glb,
cabinetTelevision.glb, rugRounded.glb, kitchenCabinet.glb,
kitchenCoffeeMachine.glb, trashcan.glb, speaker.glb, computerScreen.glb,
computerKeyboard.glb, computerMouse.glb, laptop.glb, pillow.glb,
doorway.glb, wall.glb.
The pantry adds kitchenSink.glb, kitchenCabinetUpper.glb, kitchenFridge.glb,
kitchenMicrowave.glb, tableRound.glb, stoolBar.glb and rugRectangle.glb from
the same kit, resaved with smaller vertex data. Material names are unchanged
so the office can recolor them. The stall partitions are wall.glb and the
stall doors are doorway.glb. The toy rack in the game room and the pantry
snack shelf are bookcaseOpenLow.glb, and the pillow toy reuses pillow.glb.
The other game room toys are built in code in the same toon style.
The rest of the kit is not included.
Attribution is not required; credit is still appreciated.

One More Rep: Gym & Fitness Props (free base pack)
by The Side Quest Shop

License: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source: https://thesidequestshop.itch.io/one-more-rep-gym-fitness-props

The pack ships as one pack.glb. It is split into single files:
gym-treadmill.glb (treadmill), gym-bike.glb (spin_bike), gym-dumbbells.glb
(dumbbell_rack), gym-kettlebells.glb (kb_small, kb_medium and kb_large side
by side), gym-bench.glb (flat_bench), gym-mats.glb (yoga_mats),
gym-ball.glb (stability_ball) and gym-cooler.glb (water_cooler). Each embeds
a 64x64 copy of the pack atlas recolored to the office pastels. Normals are
left out because the pack is flat shaded; the office rebuilds them on load.
gym-cooler.glb is also the pantry water cooler.
