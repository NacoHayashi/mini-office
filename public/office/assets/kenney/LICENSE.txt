Furniture Kit
by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)

License: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These GLB files are models from Kenney's Furniture Kit (official download
at kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit). The bathroom pieces (toilet, doorway,
wall, bathroomSink) keep the original material names so the office can
recolor them. bathroomSink.glb is the official GLB.
The toy rack in the game room is bookcaseOpenLow.glb, the pinch toy is
bear.glb, and the pillow toy reuses pillow.glb, all from the same kit.
The rest of the kit is not included.
Attribution is not required; credit is still appreciated.
